Privacy

Your file never leaves this browser. There is no server, no upload, no analytics, no cookies, no network request of any kind. The page is seven static files; you can read all of them, and you can run the whole thing offline by opening index.html from disk with your Wi‑Fi off. Genetic data is not revocable — treat any site that asks you to upload it accordingly.

What it computes from your file
  • Runs of homozygosity and FROH — stretches you inherited identical from both parents. Tells you whether your parents were related, and whether your background carries an endogamy signal. Consumer reports do not give you this.
  • Your maternal haplogroup, called from the mitochondrial positions on the chip, with every position it rests on shown and every branch it excludes listed.
  • Chromosomal sex and chip coverage — what your particular array can and cannot answer.
What it cannot, and will not fake
  • Ancestry percentages. These need labelled reference panels — thousands of genomes from known populations — which no raw file contains. Paste your provider's numbers in and they are labelled as your provider's estimate throughout.
  • Your paternal haplogroup. Y-SNP names do not map cleanly onto array probes, and a wrong call is worse than none. Enter it from your report and the tool does the useful part: the geography, dating and citations.
  • Chromosome painting, health, traits or relatives. Out of scope, deliberately.
